# Brand assets

A deployment's look comes from these files and `APP_NAME` in `wrangler.jsonc`. Replace them to rebrand.

- `logo.svg`: header logo, shown up to 194px wide. Keep the 1166 × 172 proportions or update `DeckBrand.tsx`.
- `app-icon.svg`, `app-icon-192.png`, `app-icon-512.png`: installed app icons, listed in `/site.webmanifest`.
- `/favicon.svg`, `/favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48 px) and `/apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px) in `public/`.
